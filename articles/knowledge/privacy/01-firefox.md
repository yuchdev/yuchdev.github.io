### Why Firefox Better

Firefox still exposes a level of privacy and user control that Blink-based browsers generally don't. You can fine-tune telemetry, fingerprinting protection, DNS over HTTPS, cookie isolation, tracking protection, "about:config" preferences, extension permissions, and many other privacy-related settings.

Whether every user utilize that level of control is another question. The important part is that the choice exists.

The more the web becomes a single-engine ecosystem dominated by Chromium/Blink, the easier it is for one company to shape not only web standards, but also the practical limits of privacy, ad blocking, and user control.

Healthy competition isn't just about performance benchmarks. It's about preventing any single vendor from becoming the de facto governor of the open web.
