Such AI helpers write ATS-friendly CVs and auto-apply to thousands of jobs. In response, human recruiters tighten their filters because they are drowning in AI-generated application slop.

So now we have AI candidate bots applying at scale, AI recruiting bots rejecting them at scale, and humans gradually disappearing from both ends of the process.

So an actual human candidate may now have a harder time reaching an actual human recruiter just to have a conversation - something that used to be the normal first step in hiring.
