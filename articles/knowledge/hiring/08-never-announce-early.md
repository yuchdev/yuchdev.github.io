The probation period works both ways. The employer is evaluating whether you fit the role, team, and company - and you are evaluating whether the role, management, culture, and expectations fit you.

That is exactly why I never "thrilled to announce" a new job to the entire internet on day one.

I prefer to wait until both sides have had enough time to understand that the cooperation actually works and that the feeling is mutual. Only then does it feel like something worth announcing, rather than celebrating a contract that is still effectively in beta.
