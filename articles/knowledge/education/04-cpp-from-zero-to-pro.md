C++ "from zero to pro" is a wonderfully optimistic title for a tutorial.

"Zero to able to write something that compiles" can probably fit into a tutorial.

"Zero to professional" is closer to 10,000 hours of programming, debugging, profiling, reading standards, studying other people's disasters, creating a few of your own, and eventually learning which 80% of C++ you should avoid in a particular project.

At that point it's no longer a tutorial. It's a career.
