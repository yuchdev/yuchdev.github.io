May I ask why a wall-climbing robot even needs a "head"?

To optimize for science fiction aesthetics while adding weight, complexity, and another vulnerable protrusion?

If there is spare payload capacity, a pair of compact manipulators would seem far more useful. For a robot designed around climbing, inspection, and maintenance, every gram and every moving part should ideally contribute to the mission - not to making the machine look vaguely humanoid.
