"Universal basic income" is a rather romantic answer if you happen to live in a wealthy, stable, democratic state with functioning institutions and a government willing to support their people.

There are plenty of places in the world where the state would tolerate mass unemployment, poverty, or people quite literally dying under bridges, because it will make them more negotiable to the role of cannon fodder or make a working camp an attractive place.

So I don't expect the social consequences of AI adoption to be painless or automatically compensated by some elegant policy response. Different societies will react very differently, and some will react badly.

And we are still near the beginning of this transition. We do not yet know how much work will be displaced, how quickly institutions will adapt, or whether they will adapt at all.

"AI will make everyone richer and UBI will solve the rest" is not a forecast. It is just one possible scenario, and a very optimistic one.
