Shipping a product was never about generating a landing page and PoC.

The first version is the easy part. The hard part starts after the first users arrive.

First you'll face dozens of UX adjustments, bug reports, feature requests, performance bottlenecks, security issues, integration problems, pricing experiments, and customer support. Even the best models can't read your customers' minds or predict every edge case.

Then comes the business itself: marketing, positioning, advertising, SEO, sales, analytics, onboarding, retention, and customer interviews. None of these disappear because your AI can generate a backend in an afternoon.

That's why "I built 20 apps this month" impresses me far less than "I have 20 paying customers"
