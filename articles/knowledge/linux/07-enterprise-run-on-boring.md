Enterprise runs on boring Linux distributions, and for good reason.

Everywhere I've worked - from finance to medtech - it has been Ubuntu/Debian or RHEL/CentOS. Must be very lucky to meet SUSE, usually where enterprise support come with it.

You never see Arch, Gentoo, or LFS in production.

Enterprises optimize for predictability: well-tested platforms, a huge knowledge base, minimal setup time, the largest hiring pool, long-term enterprise support, and someone to call when things go wrong.

They don't optimize for spending 2 am with bloodshot eyes recompiling the entire world because one package changed its ABI - while gradually becoming convinced that everything in the universe is made of Portage, including the sky, including even Linus Torvalds 😄
