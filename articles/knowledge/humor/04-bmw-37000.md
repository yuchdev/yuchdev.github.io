For $37,000 you can buy a perfectly respectable SUV - four or five seats, room for the whole family, luggage, groceries, and possibly even a bicycle.

Instead, BMW offers what looks like a bicycle that evolved a roof and forgot the second seat.

When I first saw it, I genuinely assumed it was some clever 3D-printed urban prototype costing under $2,000. Then I saw the price.

Apparently the missing seats are a premium feature.
