The only essential "functionality" of a fridge is to keep food cold. Why exactly does it need Android, an app store, telemetry, cloud accounts, and a software update policy?

The same goes for monitors. I would sincerely appreciate a genuinely "dumb" display: excellent panel, good inputs, proper color controls, no Android, no account, no recommendations, and absolutely no interest in collecting information about me.

At some point, "smart" stopped meaning "more useful" and started meaning "contains an unnecessary OS that now also needs security patches, breaches your home network and actively spies on you and your family"
