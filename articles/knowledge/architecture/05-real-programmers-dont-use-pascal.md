### REAL PROGRAMMERS DON'T USE PASCAL (old joke)

That list instantly reminded me of an old programmer meme from the early 80s.

"Real programmers don't use Pascal"

The punchline was that real programmers:
- used punch cards
- wrote only in FORTRAN or assembly
- looked down on this new, fashionable nonsense called "structured programming" 😁

Indentation, readable code, and control flow without 'goto' are just training wheels. "If you really know what you're doing, you don't need them"

Time passed - abstractions won, tools improved, and the definition of a "real programmer" quietly moved on.
I'll be probably telling to my grandkids, "our generation could write on assembly. Assembly, Karl!"
