When I was younger, I thought "open source" meant developers working for free in free time. Some people still think that - but it's far from the truth.

In reality, thousands of engineers around the world are paid to work on open-source projects like Linux. The Linux Foundation coordinates contributions from major companies - Intel, IBM, Google, Microsoft, and many others - who fund development because they rely on it.
Open source didn't become powerful despite big business involvement - it became powerful largely because of it.
