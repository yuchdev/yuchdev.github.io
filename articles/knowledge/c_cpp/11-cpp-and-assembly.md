### C++ and Assembly

I remember my first team lead at an anti-malware company reviewing CVs.

He would look at a candidate and ask very thoughtfully:
"So… you know assembly?"

(Proudly) "Yes!"

Then we'd give them a task of moderate complexity. Some could actually do it. Some - who had put "Assembly" on their CV hoping no one would check - could not.

But that wasn't the interesting part.

They would implement the task in C++, compile with -O2, and in about 90% of cases the compiler produced code faster and cleaner than their hand-written assembly ever would.

That 10% truly knew assembly.
The rest just knew the syntax.

And this is exactly what we're witnessing now with agentic AI.

Prompts are our new high-level language.
Classic programming languages are becoming our assembly.

Most people will "know the syntax."
Very few will understand what the machine is actually doing underneath.

History doesn't repeat - but it optimizes.
