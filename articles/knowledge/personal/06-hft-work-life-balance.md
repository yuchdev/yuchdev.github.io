Depends heavily on where you work.

There are HFT shops where an extreme pace is part of the culture, and the explicit contract part - markets first, personal time second. If that is the environment, then yes, the stress can be very high.

But HFT inside a large investment bank. In some places the workload is not dramatically worse than Risk, Market Data, or Infrastructure. The domain is demanding, but not necessarily chaos every day.

If work-life balance matters, I would look more toward large banks, exchanges, market infrastructure rather than the most aggressive pure HFT shops. The compensation may be lower, but the technical stack is often very similar: C++, Linux and its tuning, networking, performance profiling, lock-free/concurrent programming, latency analysis, hardware awareness and production reliability.
