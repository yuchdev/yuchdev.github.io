## A little about me

I've spent around 20 years building software across cybersecurity, finance, embedded systems, and medtech. Much of that time has gone into performance — making systems actually run faster, not just look good in design docs. Sometimes it meant rethinking an architecture and achieving 3–5× improvements. Those are still some of my favorite achievements. **A more detailed version of my CV is available** for anyone who wants to explore the projects and decisions behind this brief introduction, either upon request or on my [personal website](https://yuchdev.github.io/cv.html).

Part of my work lives at the nanosecond scale: low-level tuning, concurrency, lock-free algorithms, and the kind of optimization where you really feel the hardware pushing back. But I am just as comfortable stepping back to design a backend service, lead development, improve a build system, or make a CI pipeline reliable enough for the rest of the team to trust.

I like taking ownership of the whole engineering problem, not just the code assigned to me. I've worked across architecture, implementation, testing, production support, technical leadership, and mentoring — in large enterprises and in startups where much of the infrastructure had to be built from scratch.

## What I bring

- **C/C++ and systems engineering:** Modern C++, Boost, concurrency, lock-free programming, IPC, POSIX, Windows API, performance profiling, and cross-platform development.
- **Backend engineering:** Java, Python, distributed services, REST APIs, database integration, asynchronous processing, and production reliability.
- **High-performance applications:** Computer vision with OpenCV, GPU acceleration with NVIDIA CUDA, embedded systems, and low-latency financial software.
- **Engineering infrastructure:** CMake, CI/CD, Python automation, TeamCity, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Docker, and AWS.
- **Technical leadership:** System design, development processes, code reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentoring.
