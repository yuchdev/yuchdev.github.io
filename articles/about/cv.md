# Overview

I've spent nearly two decades as a Software Engineer and Technical Lead, designing enterprise-grade backend systems, distributed platforms, and high-performance applications across anti-malware, finance, embedded systems, and medtech.
Much of my career has revolved around performance — making systems actually run faster, not just look good in design docs.
Sometimes that meant reshaping systems entirely and achieving 3–5x improvements.
Those are still some of my favorite achievements.

Part of my work lives at the nanosecond scale, where details matter. 
I've done low-level tuning, lock-free algorithms, and architecture-specific optimizations - 
the kind where you really feel the hardware pushing back.

At the same time, I'm comfortable operating higher up the stack: system design, analytics, 
building systems that stay predictable under real load,
and CI/CD pipelines to reliably test and measure them.

I have worked across the full SDLC, including architecture design,
implementation, production support, CI/CD, technical leadership,
Agile/Scrum processes, mentoring, and maintenance of large enterprise
systems.

Beyond my professional work, I enjoy building open-source tools and educational resources. Some of these projects are actively used by the community. Here are a few that I'm particularly proud of:

* [Encrypted Information Detection](https://github.com/yuchdev/EncryptionDetect), a tool for detecting encrypted information on drives or networks

* Low-latency [URL shortener](https://github.com/yuchdev/UrlShortener)
